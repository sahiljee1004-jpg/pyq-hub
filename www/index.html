Who We Are

About VidyarthiAstra

VidyarthiAstra is a student-focused educational platform designed to make learning, exam preparation and academic information more accessible. Our mission is to empower students with the right resources, smart learning tools and timely updates, helping them move confidently towards their academic and career goals.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted digital companion for competitive exam preparation — bridging the gap between aspiration and achievement.

Our Mission

Provide free and open access to authentic PYQs, smart mock tests, and AI-driven guidance so every student gets a fair shot at success.

Platform Highlights

10,000+ verified PYQs · Smart Mock Tests · Real-time Exam Alerts · AI Academic Assistant · Fast, accessible mobile experience.
